Our Admission Process

1

Make an Enquiry

2

Complete the Application

Fill out the admission application form with the student's details.

3

Assessment / Interview

The school may arrange an assessment or interview where applicable.

4

Admission Confirmation

Successful applicants will receive information about the next steps for enrollment.

Admission Requirements

  • Completed admission application
  • Recent passport photographs
  • Previous school records where applicable
  • Birth certificate or age declaration
  • Other documents requested by the school
